How It Works

1

Link Your Playlist

Paste any YouTube playlist link, or several. Regular video links that include a playlist work too. Playlists are loaded through a public Invidious instance, with several fallbacks if one is down.

Privacy, Terms & Cookies

What we store

Everything stays in your own browser's local storage: your playlist links, your current queue, your overlay settings and your quiz best scores. We have no server and no account system, so we never receive this data.

Third-party services

  • YouTube: playlists are read through a public Invidious instance, and songs play in the YouTube player, under YouTube's Terms of Service and Google's Privacy Policy. The Invidious instance used sees which playlists you load.
  • Overlay relay: to update an overlay in OBS, the title and thumbnail of the current song are sent through the free ntfy.sh service and/or a public MQTT broker under a random, secret topic name. Turn off "Send now-playing to overlay" to stop this.
  • MQTT library: the MQTT client is loaded from jsDelivr.
  • Google Fonts: fonts are loaded from Google's servers.

Cookies

We don't set cookies. YouTube and Google Fonts may set or read their own.
